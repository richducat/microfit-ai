# microfit.AI App Store screenshot storyboard

Dominant archetype: outcome headline plus oversized real product UI.

## Frame order

1. **Train for today.**
   Readiness adapts the plan.
   UI: Today readiness and adaptive plan.

2. **Track every honest set.**
   Reps, load, rest, and effort.
   UI: Strength Foundation workout player.

3. **Useful in three minutes.**
   Timed movement when the full plan will not fit.
   UI: Micro-workout library.

4. **Guidance that stays on device.**
   No prompts or fitness history sent to a Microfit server.
   UI: Coach mode and grounded recommendation.

5. **Fuel without food math.**
   Protein, fiber, plants, and water.
   UI: Daily nutrition anchors.

6. **See the work add up.**
   Streaks, volume, history, and next steps.
   UI: Progress metrics and seven-day chart.

## Coverage

- iPhone 6.9-inch portrait: 1320 × 2868
- iPad 13-inch portrait: 2048 × 2732
- Locale: en-US

- No external photography, ratings, testimonials, prices, third-party marks, or unshipped features
